# Standing Move Free principles

Simple at the surface, intelligent underneath.

Minimize data entry; automatically collect reliable information; use structured numerical parameters; preserve notes and cues; ask adaptive followups; never treat missing clinical data as normal; keep clinicians in control; prioritize functional outcomes; show exceptions and actionable recommendations; keep mobile flows intuitive; prepare structured data for analytics and clinical learning; protect privacy and clear authorization boundaries.

Stage 2 begins as an account-specific private pilot in the existing application. No real-patient AI processing or autonomous treatment changes. Production merging/deployment requires the user's final approval after validation.
